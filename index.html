What people keep is not the gift. It is the feeling of being chosen. Every piece in this box carries the creator's own name or face.

Turning a Diwali party into a box creators keep, post and remember

A brand event invite usually gets opened once. For Nishorama × Hopshop's Diwali party, I wanted 75 creators to open a giant desi matchbox and find four Diwali rituals inside: an invitation that is also their entry ID, shagun money from the Reserve Bank of Nishorama, a phuljhadi box with their own face on it, and incense wrapped in a newspaper about respecting the women who light every home. Each piece tells its own story; together they tell the party's.

GoldenDiwali House

The PR box, file by file.

Project 01 · client: Nishorama × Hopshop. A PR box for their Diwali party, built as one big matchbox, for 75 creators (3 Nov 2026, Fielia Bombay, Mumbai). Open a folder to see each piece, its story and its graphics.

The shell · packagingRigid drawer box · 350 × 235 mm label

The matchbox.

first impression

Diwali starts with a matchstick, so the invite starts with a matchbox. The lid is a full-landscape screenprint label like a vintage Sivakasi maachis box: red rays meeting behind the creator's head, नीं roundels in the corners, brown dabu with a leopard edge. The creator's own photo breaks out of the arch, so the first thing they see is themselves on a Diwali label.

Elements

  • Screenprint rays meeting behind the head
  • Creator's real photo, head out of the arch
  • नीं corner roundels, dabu, leopard edge
  • Safety matches + creator's name on the sides

Why it works

The most ordinary Diwali object in an Indian home, made giant, premium and personal. It becomes a keepsake rather than a mailer.

Per creator

Photo and name change; all 75 lids come from one master.

Item 1 · the invitationCR80 card · 85.6 × 54 mm · two sides

The Partying Licence.

invite + compulsory entry ID

The invitation arrives as an ID card, laid out like an Indian driving licence and issued by the "Union of Nisho". It carries every detail of the night, and it is the only way in: guests show it at the door and keep it on them all night. An invite that doubles as a pass is one people keep, carry and post.

Front

  • Nishorama State Partying Licence
  • DOE 03-11-2026 · valid till: cease to exist
  • Class of Fun codes in place of vehicle classes
  • Venue, 9 PM, dress code, full address
  • Signed by Ria and Ramnek

Back

  • Legend for Class of Fun: paparazzi, tarot + tattoo, photobooth + chaat, after-dark DJ, cover room, bounce house
  • A warning in government language

Graphic language

The blush card, red band and red cog of a real licence, with Nisho brown type and a faded नीं watermark. Official at a glance, a joke on the second look.

Per creator

Name, photo and licence number NS 0311 2026 001–075.

Item 2 · the blessingNote · 156 × 66 mm · two sides

Shagun, by the Reserve Bank of Nishorama.

prosperity and luck

When someone visits an Indian home, the elders press a little money into their hand: shagun, a wish for prosperity and luck. The box does the same, with an engraved 100 from the Reserve Bank of Nishorama.

Elements

  • Engraved portrait in the oval
  • Both seals are the brown नीं logo
  • Signed by Ria, Treasurer, and Ramnek, Secretary
  • Gateway of India and straight 100s on the back

Graphic language

Intaglio lines, guilloche and engraved lettering in one brown ink on cream. A banknote's anatomy, none of a real one's marks, and "Not legal tender" on both sides.

Per creator

Serial NS 001/075 to 075/075.

Item 3 · the nostalgiaSparkler box front · 63.5 × 254 mm

Your face on the phuljhadi.

the Diwali staple, made personal

Every Indian childhood has a phuljhadi box in it: tall, loud, gaudy, with a film star on the front. We kept all of that and put the creator where the star used to be, so opening it takes them straight back to lighting sparklers on the balcony.

Elements

  • Firework cap, 3D title, script sub-title
  • A window of silver sticks
  • Litho swirl behind the creator
  • Seal, starburst, "extra long" ribbon, dabu strip

Graphic language

Maximal on purpose, like real cracker packets, kept premium by an ornate border and brand brown and gold.

Per creator

Their real photo and "Featuring" line, never a generated face.

Item 4 · the respectIncense wrap 349 × 264 mm · front page A4

The Times of Nishorama.

respect for women's dignity

The incense comes wrapped in a newspaper with one story: in every home, the first diya of Diwali is lit by a woman, and she is the one who brings out the spark and the care. The house opens with one rule at the door: respect every woman.

“She is the spark and the care. Respect her, and the whole house shines.”

On the page

  • Masthead with the नीं mark, price: respect
  • House rules: her yes is yes, her no is no, look out for each other
  • Dress code: Iraana, Ishq and Chashni, drawn from the real garments

Graphic language

A 1950s Indian broadsheet: condensed serif heads, ruled columns, a red banner, on real newsprint.
